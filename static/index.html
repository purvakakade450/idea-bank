Questions people ask

Is Idea Bank an AI idea generator?

It uses live news, search trends and reports to find real problems. AI helps turn them into ideas when it is switched on, and a human reviewer approves every new idea before it appears.

Does it prove that people will pay?

No. It shows evidence that a problem exists and gives a score. Only talking to real customers proves demand, so every idea ends with a test to run.

What do Build, Explore and Skip mean?

Build is a score of 75 or more: strong enough to start. Explore is 50 to 74: real potential, but test the weakest area first. Skip is under 50: reshape it or choose another idea.

Can I check my own idea?

Yes. Use Check my idea. Answer 13 questions and get a score, a verdict and what to test next. Nothing you type is saved.

I do not have an idea yet. Where do I start?

Open the team chatbot. It asks about your skills, time and budget, then suggests ideas that fit your team.

Will it tell my team what to build?

No. Ideas and roles are suggestions. Your team decides what to pursue.

Who approves the ideas?

Reviewers chosen by the admin read each new idea and approve or reject it. Approved ideas show who finalized them.
