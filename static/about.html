About Idea Bank

Idea Bank helps student teams find a real problem worth solving, and a business idea to explore, with the proof behind it.

What it is

A website that collects real problems from news, search trends and reports in India, and turns each one into a business idea.

Who it is for

Mixed student teams of computer, mechanical, electrical, civil and business students who want to build something together.

How ideas are checked

Every idea passes four must-have rules, gets a score out of 100, and is approved by a reviewer before it appears on the site.

What it does not do

It only suggests. Your team decides what to build, and should talk to real customers first.
